When is a cough more than just a cough?

Most coughs clear up within three weeks. Here is when a cough needs a specialist review, and what the common causes of a long-lasting cough are.

5 min readVinodhaa Respiratory Centre

Most coughs are caused by colds or viral infections and settle within three weeks. A cough that lasts longer, or comes with other symptoms, deserves a proper check rather than another bottle of cough syrup.

When to see a doctor

  • A cough lasting more than two weeks. In India, this should be checked for tuberculosis
  • Coughing up blood
  • Fever, night sweats or unexplained weight loss
  • Breathlessness or chest pain
  • A smoker whose usual cough has changed

Common causes of a long-lasting cough

  • Post-nasal drip from allergies or sinus problems
  • Asthma, sometimes with cough as the only symptom
  • Acid reflux irritating the throat
  • Certain blood pressure medicines (ACE inhibitors)
  • Smoking and COPD
  • A cough that lingers after an infection
  • Tuberculosis
  • Interstitial lung disease, and, less commonly, lung cancer

What to expect at your visit

We start with a detailed history and examination. Depending on what we find, tests may include a chest X-ray, sputum tests, lung function testing or a CT scan. The aim is to find the cause, so treatment targets the problem rather than just the symptom.

Avoid repeated antibiotics and cough syrups

Repeated courses of antibiotics or cough syrups without a diagnosis can delay the right treatment and contribute to antibiotic resistance. If your cough is not improving, it is time for a specialist review.

This page is general health information and is not a substitute for a medical consultation. If you have symptoms, please see a doctor.
