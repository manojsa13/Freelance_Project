Vinodhaa Respiratory Centre treats chest, lung and sleep conditions as its full-time practice, from chronic cough and asthma to sleep apnea and tobacco de-addiction. The clinic runs an in-house sleep study room, so diagnosis and follow-up happen without sending patients elsewhere.

What we treat.

Chest & Lung Disease

Diagnosis and treatment for chronic cough, bronchial asthma and general chest conditions.

Sleep Medicine

In-clinic sleep studies and treatment for sleep apnea, insomnia and related breathing disorders.

Interstitial Lung Disease

Management of ILD and post-infection lung fibrosis, including post-COVID recovery care.

Allergy & Immunology

Assessment and long-term care for respiratory allergies and immune-related breathing issues.

Tobacco De-addiction

Structured nicotine and tobacco cessation support alongside lung health monitoring.

General Consultation

General health and weight-related lung condition consultations for the whole family.
